An Interactive HR Employee Dashboard
# 📊 Excel Dashboards Portfolio

This repository contains interactive dashboards built with **Microsoft Excel**, showcasing data analysis, visualization, and reporting skills.  

## 📌 Projects
- **Sales Performance Dashboard** → Highlights revenue, growth trends, and product insights.  
- **HR Employee Dashboard** → Tracks workforce distribution, attrition, and performance.  
This HR Employee Dashboard was designed in Microsoft Excel to provide a comprehensive overview of workforce data. It brings together KPIs, visualizations, and interactive slicers to help HR professionals and business leaders understand employee trends, measure workforce health, and identify potential risks.

## 🔑 Key Insights
**1. Headcount Overview**

- Total Employees: 50

- Active Employees: 30 (60%)

- Resigned Employees: 20 (40%)


**2. Department Distribution**

- Marketing (11 employees) and Finance (10 employees) have the highest representation.

- Sales (6 employees) and HR (7 employees) are smaller, potentially straining these departments.

- Uneven department sizes may indicate a need for resource reallocation or new hires.

**3. Performance Ratings**

- The organization shows polarized performance:

- 14 employees with the lowest rating (1).

- 13 employees with the highest rating (5).

- This suggests a skills gap between high performers and struggling employees, pointing to training and mentoring opportunities.

**4. Employee Status**

- Active employees make up 60% of the workforce, while 40% have resigned.

- Monitoring exit reasons could help reduce turnover.

**5. Gender Distribution**

- The workforce is nearly balanced: 52% female vs 48% male.

- This is a positive indicator of gender diversity in the organization.

**6. Age Distribution**

- The 40–49 age group has the highest representation (15 employees).

- The 20–29 group is the least represented (10 employees), suggesting limited early-career hiring.

**7. Hire Trend by Year**

- Hiring peaked in 2017 (10 employees) but slowed down in subsequent years.

- Recent hires remain steady, showing consistent but cautious recruitment.

## Conclusion

The HR Employee Dashboard provides clear, actionable insights into employee distribution, attrition, diversity, and performance.

For HR Managers → It highlights areas of concern such as high attrition (40%), performance gaps, and department resource distribution.

For Business Leaders → It provides an overview of workforce demographics, enabling strategic planning around hiring, retention, and training.

For Employees → It shows a commitment to transparency and diversity by tracking metrics, such as, gender balance and age group representation.

📌 This dashboard demonstrates the power of Excel for HR analytics, combining KPIs, slicers, and interactive visualizations to support data-driven workforce decisions.

##  Recommendations.

To strengthen overall workforce effectiveness, the company should prioritize training and development, particularly by supporting low-performing employees with targeted workshops while leveraging top performers as mentors. Strategic workforce planning is also essential, with a focus on recruiting more young professionals (20–29 age group), expanding hiring in Sales and HR departments, and ensuring succession plans for senior staff. In addition, a salary benchmarking exercise is recommended to ensure pay equity across departments and genders, while aligning compensation with performance to drive motivation. Emphasis on diversity and inclusion should continue, with efforts directed toward improving representation in leadership roles and encouraging talent from diverse backgrounds. Finally, establishing a structured yearly hiring plan and addressing hiring slowdowns observed post-2017 will help the organization sustain balanced and data-driven growth across departments.
